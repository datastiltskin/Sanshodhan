### Pattern P1, "The Big Questions" box title split (extraction fault, rule)

Seen in part 2 chapters 2 (p29 to p30, note), 3 (p62, loose line), 4 (p97), 5 (p125, note), 7 (p171, loose line, the questions are glued into one paragraph "1. ... 2. ..."), 8 (p194, note, glued). Rule: join a "The Big" line with the next "Questions" line into `## The Big Questions`, drop the exercises marker, and split "1. ... 2. ..." into separate list items. ### Pattern P2, labels of drawings are broken into blocks and interleaved (extraction fault, rule)
Text set inside or around a drawing (flow charts, infographics, timelines) is emitted line by line in the order of the PDF text objects, so sentences that wrap inside a box are split and words from neighbouring boxes interleave. Seen in part 2: ch6 p144 Fig. 6.3 (four boxes), ch7 p179 (Fig. 7.11 caption glued to the Fig. 7.12 timeline labels), ch7 p185 (Fig. 7.23 caption glued to margin notes), ch8 p206 Fig. 8.18, ch1 p8 (crop calendar, all words present, order of chart). Rule: group text lines by their enclosing box and emit one block per box, and never join a line to the previous caption unless it is vertically adjacent and in the same column and italic style.

### Pattern P3, margin notes glued to figure captions (extraction fault, rule)
A margin note (blue or green side text) that is vertically near a figure caption is fused with it. Seen: ch7 p185 (E-governance note into Fig. 7.23), ch7 p186 (Patent note interleaved with Fig. 7.24 and last word "years." lost), ch8 p208 (Investment: into Fig.8.20), ch2 p40 (stray S in the box heading, same family: a graphic glyph inside a callout). Rule: margin notes are in a column left or right of the text column, so keep them out of captions by x-position.
